    What v1 can and can't see

    The tool works from down-the-line video only. It measures wrist angles, body rotation, spine posture, head and hip movement, and tempo. It can't measure your grip, your alignment relative to the target line, or your weight distribution — those signals live along the camera's line of sight and are structurally invisible from this angle. If your issue is one of those, v2 (face-on view) is the fix.
